## Introduction

[Electropedia](https://www.electropedia.org) (IEV) is the world's most
comprehensive online terminology database for electrotechnology, published by
IEC as IEC 60050. The `relaton-iev` gem resolves IEV references by extracting
and restructuring Electropedia entries from the underlying IEC 60050 data.

## Relaton components

- [Relaton implementation](https://github.com/relaton/relaton-iev)

## Prefix

To cite an IEV entry use the `IEC 60050` identifier with the relevant part
number.

> `IEC 60050-102` — Mathematical concepts

> `IEC 60050-111` — Physics and chemistry

> `IEC 60050-121` — Electromagnetism

> `IEC 60050-131` — Circuit theory

> `IEC 60050-151` — Electrical and magnetic devices
