## Introduction

The [United Nations](https://documents.un.org) publishes standards, guidelines,
and regulatory documents through its various bodies. Relaton supports citation
of UN documents from the UN Official Document System.

## Relaton components

- [Relaton implementation](https://github.com/relaton/relaton-un)

## Prefix

To cite a UN document use references prefixed with `UN`.

> `UN TRADE/CEFACT/2004/32`

> `UN TRADE/WP.4/1068`
