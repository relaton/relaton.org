## Introduction

Chinese national standards are published by the [Standardization Administration
of China](https://www.sac.gov.cn) (SAC). They include mandatory standards (GB),
recommended standards (GB/T), and sector/industry standards.

## Relaton components

- [Relaton implementation](https://github.com/relaton/relaton-gb)

## Prefixes

- `GB` — mandatory national standards

> `GB 2024-2016`

- `GB/T` — recommended national standards

> `GB/T 32843-2016`

- `GB/Z` — national standardization guiding technical documents

> `GB/Z 18509-2001`
