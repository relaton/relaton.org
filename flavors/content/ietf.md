`I-D`

> `I-D draft-abarth-cake-01`


## Additional information

The additional `IETF` prefix can be used to cite IETF documents:

>
> ```
> IETF RFC 1
> IETF BCP 3
> IETF FYI 2
> IETF STD 3
> IETF I-D draft-abarth-cake-01
> ```
