## Introduction

The [XMPP Standards Foundation](https://xmpp.org) (XSF) develops open
protocols for real-time communication based on the Extensible Messaging and
Presence Protocol (XMPP), which is an IETF standard.

## Relaton components

- [Relaton implementation](https://github.com/relaton/relaton-xsf)
- [Relaton data](https://github.com/relaton/relaton-data-xsf)

## Prefix

To cite XSF standards use the `XEP` prefix followed by the protocol number.

> `XEP 0001`

> `XEP 0384` (OMEMO Encryption)
