## Introduction

The [Internet Assigned Numbers Authority](https://www.iana.org) (IANA) is
responsible for coordinating the DNS root zone, IP addressing, and protocol
parameter registries used by Internet protocols. Relaton supports citation of
IANA registry entries.

## Relaton components

- [Relaton implementation](https://github.com/relaton/relaton-iana)
- [Relaton data](https://github.com/relaton/relaton-data-iana)

## Prefix

To cite an IANA document, use the `IANA` prefix and the registry path.

> `IANA babel`

> `IANA c-dns/c-dns-address-event-types`

> `IANA dhcpv6-parameters/dhcpv6-options-attribute`
