## Introduction

[OASIS OPEN](https://www.oasis-open.org) is a nonprofit consortium that drives
the development of open standards for security, cloud computing, and other
technology areas. Notable OASIS standards include SAML, DocBook, and DITA.

## Prefix

Prefix for all OASIS standards is `OASIS`.

> `OASIS AkomaNtosoCore-v1.0-Pt1-Vocabulary`

> `OASIS cti-stix-bindings`
