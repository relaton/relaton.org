## Introduction

The [International Telecommunication Union](https://www.itu.int) (ITU) is the
United Nations specialized agency for information and communication
technologies. Relaton supports all three ITU sectors.

## Relaton components

- [Relaton implementation](https://github.com/relaton/relaton-itu)
- [Relaton data](https://github.com/relaton/relaton-data-itu)

## Document types

Relaton supports the following ITU document types:

- `recommendation` — ITU Recommendations
- `recommendation-supplement` — Supplements to Recommendations
- `recommendation-amendment` — Amendments to Recommendations
- `recommendation-corrigendum` — Corrigenda to Recommendations
- `recommendation-errata` — Errata to Recommendations
- `recommendation-annex` — Annexes to Recommendations
- `focus-group` — Focus Group documents
- `implementers-guide` — Implementers' Guides
- `technical-paper` — Technical Papers
- `technical-report` — Technical Reports
- `joint-itu-iso-iec` — Joint ITU/ISO/IEC publications
- `resolution` — Resolutions
- `service-publication` — Service Publications
- `handbook` — Handbooks

## ITU-T

Prefix: `ITU-T` for ITU-T Standardization Sector Recommendations.

> `ITU-T L.163`

> `ITU-T G.989.2`

> `ITU-T G.989.2/Amd 1`

## ITU-R

Prefix: `ITU-R` for ITU-R Radiocommunication Sector Recommendations.

> `ITU-R BO.600-1`

Prefix: `ITU-R RR` for ITU-R Radio Regulations.

> `ITU-R RR (2020)`

## ITU-D

Prefix: `ITU-D` for ITU-D Development Sector documents.

> `ITU-D 1`
